Coaching

Guidance rooted in practice

Michael's approach to coaching is grounded in over fifteen years of Zen practice, organizational leadership at one of the West's most important Buddhist institutions, and a prior career in technology and finance. He brings a rare combination of contemplative depth and practical experience to every conversation.

Whether you are exploring meditation for the first time, navigating a leadership challenge, or moving through a significant life transition, Michael offers steady, compassionate guidance rooted in presence and honest inquiry.

Practice Guidance

One-on-one meetings for establishing or deepening a meditation practice, exploring Zen teachings, and working with the questions that arise in daily life.

Leadership Coaching

For leaders seeking to integrate mindfulness into their work, drawing on Michael's experience as President and CFO of San Francisco Zen Center.

Transition Support

Support for those moving through significant life changes — entering contemplative practice, shifting careers, or finding renewed purpose and direction.
