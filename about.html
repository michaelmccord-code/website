About

Practice, service, and presence

Sozan Michael McCord serves as President of San Francisco Zen Center, one of the most influential Zen Buddhist institutions in the West. A resident of SFZC since 2009, he was ordained as a Zen priest by Ryushin Paul Haller in 2014. His path weaves together spiritual practice, service, and organizational leadership within the Soto Zen tradition.

Michael has held a number of leadership roles at SFZC, including Director of City Center (Beginner's Mind Temple) and Chief Financial Officer. Earlier in his tenure, he served as Ino (Head of the Meditation Hall) and Tenzo (Head Cook) at Tassajara Zen Mountain Center, deepening his practice through the traditional temple positions.

Before coming to Zen practice, Michael worked in the technology and financial sectors. He holds a BA in Theology from Ambassador University and volunteered in Amman, Jordan with the Ambassador Foundation, providing vocational training for individuals with developmental disabilities.
